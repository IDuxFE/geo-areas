Phone snapshots are generated exclusively from the public upstream. No legacy manually maintained phone table is retained.

## Public release status

DataV and the ECharts example `world.json` still require a documented basis for redistributing the underlying map data. The [DataV guide](https://help.aliyun.com/zh/datav/datav-6-0/user-guide/datav-geoatlas-widget/) describes downloading boundaries and identifies AMap as its source; it does not establish a standalone redistribution license for this npm package. Public accessibility and download instructions alone do not resolve this item. Before public release, record the applicable data grant or replace these assets with sources whose redistribution terms are established. The retained ECharts 3.8.5 repository license does not establish terms for the separately hosted world example or DataV data.
